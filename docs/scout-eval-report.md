## Why the scores are perfect, and what that means

- All 17 prompts that have an intent label are copied word for word from the fixture registry's `examples`. The
  Stage-1 scorer uses the examples as seeds, so each of these prompts scores 1.0. The intent and tool metrics are
  therefore a wiring test, not an accuracy test.
- The runner does not score some things: whether multi-intent plans are correct, whether a clarification was right
  (it counts only the rate), parameter binding, and the risk level. `override_frequency` is 0 by definition, because
  the corpus has no override data.
- `clarification_rate` is 0. The two ambiguous edge cases ("Handle the Acme situation", "Do the thing we talked about
  yesterday") score below 0.40. The spec's Reject band sends them to the safe `GENERAL` intent with no tools, so the
  system does not ask a question. Prompts in the Low band (0.40-0.54) do get a clarification.
- The injection results (7 of 7 caught, 0 of 3 clean prompts blocked) are the most meaningful part, but 10 examples
  are a smoke test, not a benchmark.

## Where Pricise behaviour is tested

The Pricise registry (`scout/pricise-intents.json`) and the `pricise` pack are tested in pytest, not by this harness:

- `backend/tests/test_scout_guard.py`: hidden-instruction pages are blocked and real store text passes. The REAL
  chat, Skeptic and fee-name prompts are not blocked with clean data, and they fall back to their rules with injected
  data. The test also covers the one-warning fallback, and a hidden-instruction page that is cleaned while the check
  still completes.
- `backend/tests/test_scout_sidecar.py`: the real sidecar routes and safeguards, the same verdicts in Python and the
  sidecar, routing of Pricise intents, and `checkout.pay` always denied.
